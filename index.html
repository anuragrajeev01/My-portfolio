My Experience with STEYP

Participating in STEYP was a transformative learning experience. The platform provided a structured and engaging environment to explore core concepts in web development, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and more. Through hands-on projects like building a calculator, I was able to apply what I learned in real time, improving both my coding skills and problem-solving abilities. The mentorship and support from instructors made the journey smooth and motivating. Overall, STEYP gave me the confidence to pursue further opportunities in tech and build real-world applications with clarity and purpose.

It helped me build a strong foundation in technology and programming, and the structured approach made even complex topics easier to grasp. This experience has sparked my interest in tech and continues to guide me on my learning journey.
